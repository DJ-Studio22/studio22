What Studio 22 is

01 What this is

Studio 22 is a small arcade of original games, built by one person.

02 The line-up

Every one of these runs on the same engine.

03 Play anywhere

The same game, wherever you open it.

  • Phone

    Touch. Stick and buttons.

  • Tablet

    Touch. More room.

  • Desktop

    Keyboard. WASD or arrows.

  • Television

    Gamepad. Xbox or PlayStation.

Every game reads a gamepad, a keyboard and a touchscreen. Same code, all three. The play area is a fixed shape that scales to fit the screen. Nothing is cropped, nothing is stretched. A phone gets the same game as a television.

04 For parents

The learning games are games first.

Practice only works if it gets played twice. So these are built to be worth coming back to.

Difficulty follows the player. A streak makes it harder. A few misses ease it back. Nobody is told they are behind.

05 Privacy

We remember nothing about you, and ask for nothing in return.

  • No accounts, ever. Nothing to sign up for.
  • No ads, no purchases, no nudges to come back.
  • Nothing is collected or stored. Scores live until the tab closes.
06 Party mode

One controller. One screen. Everybody watching.

Cast the tab to the television. Pass the controller around. Everyone gets a turn and the scores stack up on screen. The list disappears when you close the tab, which is the point of it.
